# UniGPS Finance Policies

## Salary & Payroll

### Pay Schedule
- Salary is credited on the **1st of every month** to the registered bank account.
- If the 1st falls on a weekend or holiday, salary is credited on the last working day before.
- Salary breakup: Basic (40%) + HRA (20%) + Special Allowance (25%) + Other allowances (15%).

### Salary Slip
- Available on the HR portal (https://hr.unigps.in) by the 5th of each month.
- For discrepancies, raise a ticket to finance@unigps.in within 7 days.

### Bank Account
- Salary is deposited to the bank account registered during onboarding.
- To change your bank account, submit a Bank Change Request on the HR portal with a cancelled cheque.
- Processing time: 15 working days. The change applies from the next pay cycle.

## Expense Reimbursement

### Eligible Expenses
| Category | Limit | Approval |
|----------|-------|----------|
| Travel (domestic) | Actuals as per travel policy | Manager |
| Travel (international) | Actuals as per travel policy | VP + Finance |
| Client meals | INR 2,000/person/meal | Manager |
| Team events | INR 500/person | Manager |
| Books & learning | INR 5,000/year | Self-approved |
| Home office setup | INR 15,000 one-time (for WFH) | Manager |
| Mobile reimbursement | INR 1,000/month (select roles) | Auto-approved |

### How to Claim
1. Submit claims via the HR portal → Expense Claims section.
2. Attach original receipts/invoices (photo or scan).
3. Claims must be submitted **within 30 days** of the expense date.
4. Late claims (31–60 days) require manager justification. Claims older than 60 days are rejected.
5. Processing time: 7–10 working days after manager approval.
6. Reimbursement is credited with the next salary cycle.

### Travel Policy
- **Flights**: Economy class for domestic; economy/premium economy for international (>6 hours).
- **Hotels**: Up to INR 5,000/night (metro cities), INR 3,500/night (non-metro). Booked via the company travel portal.
- **Local transport**: Actuals via Uber/Ola (company account) or auto-rickshaw receipts.
- **Per diem**: INR 800/day (domestic), USD 50/day (international) for meals and incidentals.
- **Advance**: Travel advance available up to INR 50,000. Submit settlement within 7 days of return.

## Tax & Compliance

### Tax Deadlines
| Activity | Deadline |
|----------|----------|
| Investment declaration (for TDS) | April 15 |
| Investment proofs submission | January 15 |
| Form 16 issuance | June 15 |
| ITR filing deadline | July 31 (may extend per government notification) |

### Tax-Saving Investments (Section 80C)
- Eligible: PPF, ELSS, NSC, life insurance premiums, home loan principal, children's tuition.
- Maximum deduction: INR 1,50,000 per financial year.
- Submit proofs via HR portal → Tax Declaration section.

### HRA Exemption
- Employees paying rent > INR 8,333/month must submit rent receipts + landlord PAN.
- Upload monthly rent receipts on the HR portal.

### Other Deductions
- Section 80D (health insurance): Up to INR 25,000 (self) + INR 25,000 (parents).
- Section 80E (education loan interest): No limit on deduction amount.
- NPS (Section 80CCD): Additional INR 50,000 deduction.

## Invoicing & Payments

### Vendor Payments
- All vendor invoices must be submitted to finance@unigps.in with the PO number.
- Payment terms: Net 30 days from invoice date (unless contract specifies otherwise).
- Invoices without a valid PO will be returned for correction.

### Client Invoicing
- Client invoices are raised on the 1st of each month for the previous month's services.
- Payment tracking: Check status on the Finance dashboard (https://finance.unigps.in).
- Overdue payments (>45 days) are escalated to the account manager.

## Company Credit Card

- Issued to employees at Manager level and above.
- Monthly limit: INR 1,00,000 (can be increased with VP approval).
- All charges must be reconciled on the HR portal within 15 days of statement date.
- Personal use of the company credit card is strictly prohibited.
