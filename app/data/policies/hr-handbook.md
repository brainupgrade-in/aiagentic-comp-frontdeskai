# UniGPS HR Handbook — Gheware UniGPS Solutions LLP

## Leave Policy

### Casual Leave (CL)
- Entitlement: 24 casual leaves per calendar year (Jan–Dec).
- Accrual: 2 CLs credited per month on the 1st.
- Carry-forward: Up to 5 unused CLs carry over to the next year; the rest lapse on Dec 31.
- Minimum duration: Half-day (0.5 day).
- Maximum consecutive: 5 days without manager approval; 6–10 days require manager approval; >10 days require HR Head approval.
- Notice: Apply at least 2 working days in advance for planned leave.

### Sick Leave (SL)
- Entitlement: 12 sick leaves per year.
- Medical certificate required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.
- Sick leave cannot be used for planned vacations.

### Earned/Privilege Leave (EL)
- Entitlement: 15 earned leaves per year, available after completing 6 months of service.
- Must be applied at least 7 days in advance.
- Can be carried forward up to a maximum of 30 days.
- Encashment: Unused EL beyond 30 days is encashed at basic salary rate during year-end settlement.

### Maternity Leave
- 26 weeks for the first two children as per Maternity Benefit Act.
- Apply at least 8 weeks before expected delivery date.
- Contact HR for detailed documentation requirements.

### Paternity Leave
- 5 working days within one month of the child's birth.

## Work From Home (WFH) Policy

- Eligible employees: All full-time employees who have completed their probation period.
- Entitlement: 2 WFH days per week (Tuesday and Thursday are default WFH days).
- Additional WFH days require manager approval via the HR portal.
- Employees must be available on all communication channels (Slack, email, phone) during work hours (9:30 AM – 6:30 PM IST).
- WFH is not applicable during the first week of onboarding or during critical project milestones unless approved.

## Attendance & Working Hours

- Standard working hours: 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST, Monday to Friday.
- Lunch break: 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM (1 hour).
- Core hours: 10:30 AM to 4:30 PM — all employees must be available during core hours.
- Flexi-time: Employees may start between 8:30 AM and 10:30 AM with manager approval.
- Overtime: Not applicable for salaried employees. Contract employees follow separate overtime rules.

## Insurance & Benefits

### Health Insurance
- Group health insurance coverage for all full-time employees and dependents (spouse + 2 children).
- Sum insured: INR 5,00,000 per family per year.
- Coverage includes hospitalization, day-care procedures, pre- and post-hospitalization (30/60 days).
- Dental and vision: Not covered under standard plan. Optional top-up available at employee cost.
- Claims process: Submit claims via the HR portal within 30 days of discharge.

### Term Life Insurance
- Coverage: 3x annual CTC for all confirmed employees.
- Nominee details must be updated in the HR portal.

## Onboarding

- Probation period: 6 months for all new hires.
- During probation: No earned leave, WFH limited to 1 day/week.
- Confirmation: Automatic upon successful completion unless HR communicates otherwise.
- Buddy program: Each new hire is assigned a buddy for the first 3 months.
- Equipment: Laptop and accessories issued on Day 1. Return required on last working day.

## Performance & Appraisals

- Appraisal cycle: April to March (annual).
- Mid-year review: October.
- Rating scale: 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Exceptional).
- Increment effective date: April 1st each year.
- Promotion eligibility: Minimum 18 months in current role with rating >= 4.

## Separation & Exit

- Notice period: 60 days for confirmed employees; 30 days during probation.
- Notice period buyout: Allowed at company discretion, calculated at gross salary rate.
- Exit interview: Mandatory, conducted by HR in the last week.
- Full and final settlement: Within 45 days of last working day.
- Experience letter: Issued within 7 days of full settlement.

## Code of Conduct

- All employees must adhere to the Anti-Harassment Policy (see separate document).
- Dress code: Business casual on weekdays; casual on Fridays.
- Conflict of interest: Employees must declare any outside employment or consulting engagements.
- Confidentiality: All company data, client information, and source code are strictly confidential.
