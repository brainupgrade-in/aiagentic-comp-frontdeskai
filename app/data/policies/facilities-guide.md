# UniGPS Facilities & Administration Guide

## Office Location

- **Address**: UniGPS Solutions LLP, 4th Floor, Tower B, Prestige Tech Park, Outer Ring Road, Bangalore 560103.
- **Working hours**: Office is accessible 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM, Monday to Saturday.
- **After-hours access**: Requires security approval. Submit request via admin@unigps.in at least 24 hours in advance.

## Desk & Seating

### Hot Desking
- Floors 4A and 4B follow hot-desking policy (no assigned seats).
- Book a desk via the UniGPS Facilities App (iOS/Android) or https://facilities.unigps.in.
- Bookings open at 6:00 PM the previous day.
- Cancel unused bookings by 10:00 AM to avoid a no-show penalty.

### Permanent Desks
- Team leads and above have assigned desks on Floor 4C.
- Desk assignment changes require manager and admin approval.
- Standing desk conversion available on request (INR 3,000 deposit, refundable).

### Meeting Rooms
| Room | Floor | Capacity | Equipment |
|------|-------|----------|-----------|
| Everest | 4A | 20 | Projector, whiteboard, video conferencing |
| K2 | 4A | 10 | TV screen, whiteboard |
| Nanda Devi | 4B | 6 | TV screen |

- Book via Google Calendar (add room as a resource) or the Facilities App.

## Parking

### Two-Wheeler Parking
- Basement Level 1. No booking required.
- Helmet must be carried to your desk (no storage in parking).

### Four-Wheeler Parking
- Basement Level 2. Limited spots — first-come, first-served.
- Monthly parking pass: INR 2,000/month. Apply via admin@unigps.in.
- Visitor parking: 2 spots available near the main entrance. Inform security in advance.

### Electric Vehicle Charging
- 4 EV charging stations in Basement Level 2.
- Charging is free for the first 2 hours; INR 50/hour after that.
- Book a slot via the Facilities App.

## Cafeteria

- **Location**: Ground Floor, Tower B.
- **Hours**: Breakfast 8:30–10:00 AM, Lunch 12:30–2:00 PM, Snacks 4:00–5:00 PM.
- **Subsidized meals**: Lunch at INR 50/meal (company subsidizes the rest).
- **Payment**: Deducted from salary based on usage (swipe your ID card).
- **Dietary options**: Vegetarian, non-vegetarian, and vegan options available daily. Menu posted weekly on the Facilities App.
- **Pantry**: Tea, coffee, water, and biscuits available 24/7 on each floor.

## Access Cards & Security

### ID Card / Access Card
- Issued on Day 1 of joining.
- Provides access to: main entrance, your assigned floor, gym, cafeteria.
- **Lost card**: Report immediately to admin@unigps.in. Replacement issued within 1 working day (INR 500 charge).
- **Temporary pass**: Available from the security desk for visitors or employees who forgot their card.

### Visitor Management
- All visitors must be pre-registered via the Facilities App or email to admin@unigps.in.
- Visitor receives a temporary badge at reception.
- Visitors must be escorted at all times beyond the reception area.
- Maximum visit duration: 8 hours.

## Building Maintenance

### Reporting Issues
- **AC/heating**: Report via Facilities App → Maintenance → HVAC.
- **Electrical** (lights, power outlets): Report via Facilities App → Maintenance → Electrical.
- **Plumbing** (restrooms, water coolers): Report via Facilities App → Maintenance → Plumbing.
- **Cleaning**: Report via Facilities App or inform the floor housekeeping staff.
- **Emergency** (fire, water leak, power outage): Call security ext. 9999 immediately.

### Response Times
| Type | Response Time |
|------|---------------|
| Emergency | Immediate |
| HVAC issue | 2 hours |
| Electrical | 4 hours |
| Plumbing | 4 hours |
| Cleaning request | 1 hour |
| Furniture request | 2 business days |

## Gym & Recreation

- **Gym**: Ground Floor, open 6:00 AM – 9:00 PM.
- Free for all employees. Towels and lockers available.
- Personal trainer available (Mon/Wed/Fri 7:00–8:00 AM) at INR 2,000/month.
- **Recreation room**: Table tennis, foosball, and board games on Floor 4B.
- **Quiet room**: Floor 4C, Room 410 — for meditation, prayer, or rest. No talking/phone calls.

## Stationery & Supplies

- Basic stationery (pens, notepads, sticky notes) available at the supply cabinet on each floor.
- For bulk orders or special items, submit a request to admin@unigps.in.
- Printing: Printers on each floor. Use your ID card to authenticate. Color printing requires admin approval for >50 pages.
